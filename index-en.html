About Tide Mobile

Before we design a feature, we do not ask "What does it do?" We ask "How does it affect a person?"

Tide Mobile is an independent mobile app studio based in Adana, Türkiye. From mental health to travel planning, from financial awareness to cognitive therapy, we build apps that genuinely make everyday life easier.

We bring behavioral psychology, user research, data analysis and design under one roof. We first understand the behavior and the need, validate the opportunity, then build the product. Our psychology content is approved by a licensed psychologist.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions.

What is Tide Mobile?

Tide Mobile (Tidemobile) is an independent mobile app studio based in Adana, Türkiye. It builds Modi, a mental health app, and TerraVisa, a travel and visa planning app, combining psychology, data and design.

Which apps does Tide Mobile make?

Modi (mental health, live on the App Store and Google Play), TerraVisa (visa and travel planning, live on Google Play, iOS version in App Store review), LunaPara (financial awareness, in closed testing) and Reframe (CBT-based thought records, in development).

What is Modi and is it free?

Modi is a mental health app with a mood journal, guided meditations, psychologist-approved journeys, short self-check tests and an AI chat companion. It is free to download and its core features are free; an optional Modi Premium adds more content. It is available on iOS and Android in Turkish and English. Learn more on Modi's website.

Does Modi replace therapy?

No. Modi does not replace therapy and does not diagnose. It is a tool that supports everyday well-being. When the chat detects risk, it directs users to professional support such as emergency services.

What is TerraVisa and is it free?

TerraVisa is a travel app that shows visa requirements for your passport and political risk levels on an interactive world map. It includes a Schengen 90/180 day calculator, a visa cost calculator, a document wizard and VFS/BLS consulate appointment alerts. The app is free; an optional premium subscription adds extra features.

How can I track the Schengen 90/180 day rule?

You can stay in the Schengen area for at most 90 days within any 180-day period. Add your entry and exit dates to TerraVisa's Schengen 90/180 calculator and it works out your remaining days automatically.

Where can I download TerraVisa?

TerraVisa is free on Google Play for Android. The iOS version is in App Store review.

When will LunaPara be public?

LunaPara is currently in closed testing. For early access, join the test group and become a tester on Google Play.
