Stonehenge

Wiltshire, England, United Kingdom

About

According to UNESCO, Stonehenge and Avebury, in Wiltshire, are among the most famous groups of megaliths in the world. The two sanctuaries consist of circles of menhirs arranged in a pattern whose astronomical significance is still being explored. These holy places and the nearby Neolithic sites are an incomparable testimony to prehistoric times.

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Stonehenge, Avebury and Associated Sites

Visit Tips

Don’t miss
  • The Stonehenge stone circle
  • The Avebury stone circles, part of the same World Heritage site
  • The surrounding Neolithic sites
Getting there

Salisbury is the nearest train station, with a direct line from London Waterloo taking around 1h30m. From Salisbury, take a taxi (12 miles/19 km) or the Stonehenge Tour Bus.
