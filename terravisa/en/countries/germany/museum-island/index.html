Museum Island

Berlin, Germany

About

Museum Island (Museumsinsel) in the heart of Berlin is made up of five museums of outstanding historical and artistic importance. Built between 1824 and 1930 by the most renowned Prussian architects of their time, they reflect how approaches to museum design evolved over that century. Museum Island has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1999.

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Museumsinsel (Museum Island), Berlin
