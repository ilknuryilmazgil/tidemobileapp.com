Hallstatt

Salzkammergut, Austria

About

In the Hallstatt-Dachstein area of the Salzkammergut, part of the Eastern Alps, huge mountains rise abruptly from narrow valleys. Its prosperity since medieval times has been based on salt mining around the town of Hallstatt, whose name means salt settlement. Systematic salt production in the region dates back to the Middle Bronze Age, in the late 2nd millennium BC. The Hallstatt-Dachstein / Salzkammergut Cultural Landscape has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1997.

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Hallstatt-Dachstein / Salzkammergut Cultural Landscape
