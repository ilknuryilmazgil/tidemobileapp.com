Florence

Tuscany, Italy

About

It grew on the site of an Etruscan settlement and the Roman colony of Florentia, founded in 59 BC. In the 15th and 16th centuries, during the early Medici period, it became a symbol of the Renaissance. The River Arno runs through the city, and the bridges linking its banks include the Ponte Vecchio. The Historic Centre of Florence has been on the UNESCO World Heritage List since 1982.

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Historic Centre of Florence
