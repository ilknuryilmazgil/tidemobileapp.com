General Information

This Privacy Policy explains why and how your personal information is processed through the TerraVisa website (tidemobileapp.com/terravisa) and the TerraVisa mobile app, who it is shared with, and what your rights are.

TerraVisa is developed and published by Tide Mobile (Adana, Türkiye). Tide Mobile is the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy has been prepared with the information obligations of Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) in mind; the rights of users in the European Union under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are also set out below.

What Information We Process

On the website

  • Your passport choice: The passport you choose on country pages is stored only in your browser so it can be remembered on your next visit; it is not sent to us.
  • Contact form: If you use the form, your name, email address, chosen subject and message.
  • Country Stories submissions: If you submit a story, its title and text, the country you visited, the type of trip, the name to be shown on your story, your email address and any photos you add. Photos are reprocessed on our server and stripped of hidden data such as location. To prevent misuse, your IP address is not stored; only an irreversible summary (hash) of it is kept. Your email address is never published.
  • Connection information: The provider that hosts our website records the IP addresses of devices connecting to it for security purposes.

Our website does not use cookies, visitor analytics tools or advertising.

In the app

  • Account information: If you create an account, your name and email address. Your password is stored securely and cannot be seen by us.
  • Information that stays on your device: Your travel plans, saved content, profile photo and app preferences (such as language and passport) are stored on your device. Access to your photos is requested only so you can choose a profile photo; the photo you choose does not leave your device.
  • Notifications: If you turn notifications on, your device’s notification address, the countries you follow, your passport country, your language and your notification preferences are stored on our servers, linked to your account, so we can send you notifications.
  • Community appointment reports: If you share an appointment report, its content (such as the centre, country, visa type and date window) is stored together with a user number linked to your account. Confirming or flagging reports is also recorded with this number.
  • Purchases: Premium subscriptions are bought through the store you downloaded the app from (App Store or Google Play); we do not see your payment details. To verify your subscription, your purchase records and an app-specific ID are processed by our subscription management provider.
  • Translation: When you use the in-app translation feature, the text you want translated is sent to our translation provider.
  • Public information: Public content such as news, weather, exchange rates and country information is retrieved from the relevant services. No information identifying you is sent with these requests, although by the nature of the connection these services can see your device’s IP address.
  • Advertising infrastructure: The app includes Google’s advertising infrastructure, which starts when the app opens. When ads are shown, they are delivered through it. Through this infrastructure Google may process device identifiers and technical information about app usage.

What we do not collect: TerraVisa never asks for or stores passports, ID documents, visa numbers, booking codes or similar documents. Your location is not collected.

Why We Use Information

  • To create your account, manage your session and protect your account,
  • To send you the notifications you have turned on,
  • To show community appointment reports and prevent misuse,
  • To verify your Premium subscription,
  • To answer your enquiries,
  • To review the stories you submit and publish those that are approved,
  • To keep the website and app running securely,
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Legal Bases

Your personal data is processed on the following legal bases under Article 5 of the KVKK:

  • Entering into or performing a contract: providing the account, notification and subscription services.
  • Legitimate interest of the controller: service security, preventing misuse and keeping community reports reliable.
  • Legal obligation: meeting obligations arising from legislation.
  • Establishing, exercising or protecting a right: protecting our rights in possible disputes.

Notifications depend on you granting notification permission on your device and turning notifications on in the app; you can turn them off at any time. For users covered by the GDPR, the corresponding legal bases are performance of a contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests.

Who We Share It With

To provide the service, your information is processed by the following providers, only to the extent necessary:

  • Google (Firebase): account management, server and data storage infrastructure; storing story submissions and their photos.
  • Google: the app’s advertising infrastructure.
  • Expo: delivering notifications to your device.
  • RevenueCat: managing subscription status.
  • DeepL: the translation feature.
  • Apple and Google: app stores and payments.
  • GitHub: hosting the website.
  • Formspree: forwarding the website’s contact form to us.

These providers’ servers may be located abroad, so your information may be processed outside Türkiye. Information may be shared with authorised public bodies when they make a lawful request, within the framework of the law.

How Long We Keep It

  • Account information: kept until you delete your account.
  • Notification record: deleted when you turn notifications off or delete your account in the app.
  • Community appointment reports: no longer shown in the app 2 hours after they are shared. To have report records linked to your account deleted, write to privacy@tidemobileapp.com.
  • Story submissions: Unpublished submissions are deleted automatically, with their photos, within 180 days at the latest. Published stories stay online under the name you chose until you ask for them to be removed; to request this, write to .
  • Contact messages: kept in our mailbox; we delete them on request.
  • Information on your device: deleted when you uninstall the app. Your passport choice on the website is deleted when you clear your browser data.

Data Security

Connections between the app and our servers, and to our website, are encrypted. Only authorised people can access the information on our servers, and your password cannot be seen by us. Even so, no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information if it is, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad, to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate, to request deletion or destruction where the conditions are met, to request that these actions be notified to the third parties it was transferred to, to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Users covered by the GDPR also have the right to data portability, to restriction of processing and to object to processing.

You can send requests to privacy@tidemobileapp.com. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can apply to Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Board, or, if you live in the European Union, to your country’s data protection authority.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app at any time or send us a deletion request by email. The steps and what is deleted are explained on the Delete Account page.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the law, our services or the providers we use change. The current version is always published on this page, and the “Last updated” date at the top changes.

Contact

For questions about privacy and your personal data, write to privacy@tidemobileapp.com; for anything else, write to tidemobile.contact@gmail.com or use our Contact page.

Data controller: Tide Mobile, Adana, Türkiye

Important Note

TerraVisa is a platform that provides general information about travel and visa processes. It does not submit visa applications, is not an official application representative and never asks its users for sensitive documents such as passports or ID.
