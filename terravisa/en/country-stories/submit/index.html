    Submission Rules

    • The story must be an original experience of your own.
    • Use accurate, respectful and constructive language.
    • Do not use text copied from other sources.
    • You must own the copyright of your photos.
    • Do not share photos of passports, ID cards, tickets or similar documents.
    • Do not share other people’s faces or personal details without permission.
    • Information about visa and entry rules is checked by our editors against official sources; unverifiable information is not published.

    Moderation Process

    1. 1Submission ReceivedYour story is stored securely; it is never published automatically.
    2. 2ReviewOur editorial team checks the content, photos and accuracy of information. Stories may be shortened or corrected if needed.
    3. 3Approval and PublicationApproved stories are published in Country Stories under the name you chose. Your email address is never shown.

    About Your Submission

    Not every submission will be published. Unpublished submissions are deleted within 180 days. To have your submission deleted, write to privacy@tidemobileapp.com.
