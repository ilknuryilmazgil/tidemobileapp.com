Do I need a visa for Lithuania with an Australian passport?

Short answerNo visa needed for short trips
Entry type

The Schengen 90/180 rule

Lithuania is in the Schengen Area. For short stays you can spend up to 90 days in any 180-day period across all Schengen countries together. Entry and exit days both count.
