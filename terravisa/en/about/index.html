About TerraVisa

We make planning trips, exploring and getting to know the world easier and more accessible for everyone.

  • Travel TechnologyA travel-focused app and site
  • Official SourcesContent with cited sources
  • User-FocusedDesigned around your needs
  • Accessible TravelClear information for everyone

Our Mission

To bring reliable, up-to-date travel and visa information based on official sources together in one platform, making people’s journeys of discovery better planned, safer and more inspiring.

Our Vision

To be the trusted, pioneering platform that comes to mind for travel, visa and destination information.

Our Values

  • ReliabilityWe provide verified, up-to-date information.
  • AccessibilityWe make complex information simple and clear for everyone.
  • User FocusWe build our products and content around users’ needs.
  • Discovery and InspirationBeyond information, we inspire new routes and experiences.

What Is TerraVisa?

TerraVisa is a travel platform for everyone planning a journey, with visa and entry information, country guides, travel articles and trip planning tools.

  • Entry information by passport for 31 countries
  • Country guides and must-see places
  • Practical tools for trip planning
  • Articles based on official sources in TerraVisa Journal
