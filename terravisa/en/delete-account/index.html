Delete Account

How to delete your TerraVisa account and what is deleted.

  • In the AppDelete in a few steps
  • By EmailIf you can’t access the app
  • PermanentYour account data is deleted
  • 30 DaysRequests done within 30 days

On This Page

  1. Deleting in the App
  2. Requesting Deletion by Email
  3. What Is Deleted
  4. Your Premium Subscription

1. Deleting in the App

  1. Open the Profil (Profile) screen in the TerraVisa app.
  2. Tap Hesap Ayarları (Account Settings).
  3. Tap Hesabı Sil (Delete Account) and confirm.

For security reasons the app may ask you to sign in again; in that case, sign out, sign back in and repeat the steps.

2. Requesting Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, send an email with the subject “Account deletion request” to privacy@tidemobileapp.com from the email address registered to your account. After verifying your request, we will delete your account and let you know. Requests are completed within 30 days at the latest.

3. What Is Deleted

  • Your account information (name and email address) is permanently deleted.
  • The notification preferences linked to your account are deleted.
  • Your travel plans, saved content and profile photo are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • If you also want the community appointment reports you shared to be deleted, say so in your email request.

4. Your Premium Subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a subscription you started through a store. You need to cancel it in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account.
