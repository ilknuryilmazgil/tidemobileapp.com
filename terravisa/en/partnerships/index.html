Partnership Areas

We primarily consider partnerships in the following areas:

  • Urban mobility: e-scooter, taxi and car rental services
  • Travel insurance
  • Accommodation
  • Tourism offices and public institutions
  • Travel-related digital services

What TerraVisa Offers

TerraVisa is a travel app that gives international travellers information on visas, entry requirements and local transport, based on official sources.

  • Visa and entry information verified against official sources
  • Content tailored to passports from Türkiye, the USA and the 27 EU countries
  • Country and city guides covering transport, life and culture
  • Trip planning tools

International users: We are adding English language support to TerraVisa. The app will serve international users in Turkish and English, and we plan to add more languages over time.

Our Partnership Principles

  • Legal authorisation: We only work with organisations and companies that hold the legal permits required for their activity.
  • Clear labelling: Partner content is clearly marked as “Partner” in the app.
  • Impartial information: Visa, entry and safety information is not influenced by partnerships and is based only on official sources.
  • User privacy: We do not share our users’ personal data with partners.

Partnership Models

  • Featuring your service in the relevant section of TerraVisa
  • Directing users straight to your service or app
  • Exclusive campaigns or discount codes for TerraVisa users
  • Performance-based partnerships built on trackable referrals

We prefer to start new partnerships with a limited, measurable pilot.

Get in Touch

Send your partnership proposal to tidemobile.contact@gmail.com with the subject “Partnership”. Including your organisation’s name, the service you offer, the cities you operate in and the partnership model you propose helps us review it faster. Proposals in areas other than those listed above are also considered.

The information you share by email is used only to review your proposal; see our Privacy Policy for details.
